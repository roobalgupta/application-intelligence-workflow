# Gap Analysis and Factuality Check

This is the final quality control stage.

The purpose is to identify what prevents the application from becoming stronger while ensuring that every improvement remains truthful and defensible.

# Part 1: Gap Analysis

Identify every meaningful gap between the candidate's final CV and a highly competitive application for the target vacancy.

For each gap provide:

## What Is Missing?

Describe the missing requirement, evidence or positioning.

## Why Does It Matter?

Explain its importance for this specific vacancy.

## What Type of Gap Is It?

Classify it as:

* Genuine experience gap
* Evidence gap
* Presentation gap
* Keyword gap
* Seniority perception gap
* Industry familiarity gap
* Sponsorship or work authorisation barrier
* Other practical hiring barrier

## What Could Improve It?

Identify genuine existing evidence that might strengthen the application.

If the candidate may have relevant experience that has not yet been captured, explain exactly what should be checked or confirmed.

## Suggested Improvement

Provide proposed CV wording only where supporting evidence exists.

Never fabricate experience to close a gap.

# Part 2: Candidate Confirmation Questions

Where additional genuine experience could materially improve the application, ask targeted questions.

Examples include:

Did you personally own this activity or contribute to it?

Can the result be quantified?

What was the scale?

How many users, customers, projects, transactions or stakeholders were involved?

Did you make the decision or execute another person's decision?

Which tools did you personally use?

Was this your individual result or a team result?

Did the outcome affect revenue, cost, efficiency, quality, adoption, risk or customer experience?

Did you manage people directly?

Did you lead stakeholders without formal management authority?

Can the result be defended confidently in an interview?

Only incorporate new information after the candidate confirms it.

# Part 3: Sponsorship Recheck

Before finalising the application, verify that no new information changes the sponsorship assessment.

Confirm:

* Employer sponsor licence result
* Specific vacancy sponsorship wording
* Any right to work restriction
* Current Skilled Worker role viability
* Any material salary concern
* Any material occupation eligibility concern
* Any uncertainty that should remain visible

Do not convert uncertainty into a positive result without evidence.

If sponsorship has become clearly impossible, flag this before further application work.

# Part 4: Factuality Review

Review every major claim in the tailored CV against the candidate's source information.

Check:

* Job titles
* Employers
* Employment dates
* Education
* Qualifications
* Certifications
* Metrics
* Budgets
* Revenue
* Team sizes
* Tools
* Platforms
* Technical capabilities
* Management responsibility
* Geographic scope
* Industry claims
* Customer exposure
* Project ownership
* Commercial outcomes
* Research outcomes
* Portfolio projects

# Attribution Check

Ensure the CV correctly distinguishes between:

* Owned
* Led
* Managed
* Built
* Designed
* Delivered
* Implemented
* Contributed
* Supported

Also distinguish:

* Individual result
* Team result
* Department result
* Company result

Do not combine these in ways that exaggerate responsibility.

# Interview Defensibility Test

For every major claim, ask:

Could the candidate explain exactly what they did?

Could they explain how the result was measured?

Could they explain their personal contribution?

Could they explain the tools or methods used?

Could they answer detailed follow up questions without contradicting the CV?

If not, revise the claim.

# Final Application Check

Before considering the CV ready, confirm:

* Sponsorship verification has been completed
* No explicit sponsorship barrier has been ignored
* The first half of page one explains why the candidate fits
* The strongest evidence is visible
* The highest priority requirements are addressed
* Important keywords appear naturally
* The CV demonstrates appropriate seniority
* Claims are supported
* Metrics are credible
* Job titles remain accurate
* There is no invented experience
* There is no unnecessary repetition
* The document is concise
* The candidate could defend the application confidently in an interview

# Final Output

Provide:

1. Final Sponsorship Status
2. Most important remaining professional gaps
3. Improvements that can be made immediately
4. Questions requiring candidate confirmation
5. Claims that should be softened or removed
6. Final recommended CV changes
7. Final application risks that remain unresolved

# Final Rule

A missing requirement is better than a fabricated requirement.

A clear sponsorship uncertainty is better than a false sponsorship assumption.

The final application should maximise credible evidence while keeping genuine limitations visible.
