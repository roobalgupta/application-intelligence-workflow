# Application Intelligence Workflow

A role agnostic AI workflow for evaluating job opportunities, analysing job descriptions, mapping verified candidate evidence, tailoring CVs and challenging the final application from a recruiter perspective.

Built primarily around UK job applications, the framework is designed so that the methodology stays fixed while the candidate's professional context changes.

A marketer, data scientist, software engineer, product manager, finance professional or candidate from another field can use the same workflow by replacing only their own career information, CV and target role.

## Why This Exists

Most AI CV workflows begin with something like:

> Paste your CV.  
> Paste the job description.  
> Rewrite the CV.

This workflow starts earlier.

Before changing the CV, it asks:

> Is this role actually worth applying for?

It then works through the application in a structured sequence:

1. Understand the candidate
2. Assess whether the opportunity is realistic
3. Analyse what the employer is actually hiring for
4. Identify the most important requirements
5. Map those requirements against verified candidate evidence
6. Tailor the CV using that evidence
7. Review the result from a recruiter perspective
8. Compare the candidate against realistic competition
9. Identify remaining gaps
10. Check every important claim for factual accuracy

The objective is not to make every candidate look suitable for every vacancy.

The objective is to identify genuine fit and communicate it as strongly and credibly as possible.

## Core Principle

**Fixed workflow. Variable professional context.**

The candidate does not need to design their own CV evaluation methodology.

They provide the information that is personal to them.

The framework handles the analysis.

### The candidate provides

* Their professional background
* Their current career level
* Their target roles
* Their target seniority
* Their preferred industries
* Their location preferences
* Their salary expectations
* Their employment preferences
* Their work authorisation situation where relevant
* Their personal application constraints
* Their Master CV
* Additional verified experience
* The job description they want to assess

### The workflow determines

* Whether the role is worth pursuing
* The most important requirements in the job description
* Which requirements are essential
* Which requirements are preferred
* What signals seniority in that specific role
* Which candidate evidence is most relevant
* Where genuine experience gaps exist
* How the CV should be prioritised
* Which terminology should appear naturally
* Whether the final CV reads at the right level
* How competitive the application appears
* Which claims require clarification or correction

The candidate does not need to manually identify these things before using the workflow.
